Event Ninja

A local event digest and guide for live music, festivals, fairs, outdoor markets, comedy, and community happenings across Moncton, Dieppe, Shediac, Bouctouche, and nearby SE New Brunswick towns.

What this site is

Event Ninja aggregates dated public events from city calendars, venue listings, and local sources, then publishes twice-daily digests so residents and visitors can see what’s on. This public homepage is the application presence for partner APIs and affiliate programs (including Ticketmaster Discovery).

Coverage

  • Primary hub: Moncton, Dieppe, Shediac, Bouctouche
  • Also nearby: Riverview, Memramcook, Cap-Pelé / Cap-Acadie, Grande-Digue, Cocagne, Sackville, Petitcodiac, Dorchester, Scoudouc, Richibucto, Saint-Louis-de-Kent, Alma / Fundy Shore, and other SE NB communities with public dated events
  • Flagship venues tracked: Pays de la Sagouine, Akadi Lumina, Capitol Theatre, Dieppe Arts & Culture Centre, and more
